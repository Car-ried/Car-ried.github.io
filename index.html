    Custom Cold-Air Intake

    Designed and built a custom carbon fibre intake for a Mk7.5 Golf GTI, developing skills in Fusion 360 forms, product design, 3D scanning, and composite fabrication.

    MMSF Tensile Load Limiter

    Metal Matrix Syntactic Foams (MMSF's) are useful for their energy absorption capabilities. This testing apparatus was designed to integrate a shear pin mechanism allowing for the precise control of an activation threshold.
